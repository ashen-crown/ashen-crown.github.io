Privacy policy

Ashen Crown ("we") collects only what the website needs and deletes it when the purpose is done.

Effective 2026-11-11

1. What we collect and why

WhenWhatWhy
Playtest sign-up formRequired: nickname, email
Optional: your PC, favorite genres, message		To send test dates and instructions, and understand test hardware
Email to usYour email address and messageTo reply
Visit statistics (only if you allow)Google Analytics cookies, pages viewed, rough location, device typeTo see which content is read and improve the site

Children under 14 may not sign up for playtests.

2. How long we keep it

  • Playtest sign-ups: deleted within 30 days after that playtest ends.
  • Emails: kept for one year after we reply, then deleted.
  • Visit statistics: Google Analytics retention setting (14 months).

We never sell or hand your data to others. We use these overseas services to run the site and email:

4. Cookies and visit statistics

On your first visit we ask whether we may use analytics cookies. Google Analytics runs only if you choose Allow, with IP addresses shortened. The site works the same if you decline.

Change your analytics choice

5. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or ask us to stop using it. Email ashencrown.support@gmail.com and we will act within 10 days.

6. Keeping it safe

  • Sign-ups and emails are kept in an account only the operator can access, protected with two-step verification.
  • Data is deleted within the periods above.

7. Contact

Privacy contact: Ashen Crown operator, ashencrown.support@gmail.com (reply within 2 business days, Korean time).

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the date and what changed on this page.
