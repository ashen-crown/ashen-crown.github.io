2. What others see in chat, reports and blocks

  • Chat opens at account level 10. Your chat name and messages are visible to other players in that room. Direct messages are visible only to you and the other player.
  • The server checks every message first and blocks profanity, personal information (such as phone numbers) and advertising. Repeated hits lead to an automatic, temporary chat ban.
  • You can report any message and block its sender at the same time. A message reported by three different players is hidden until we review it.
  • We review reported messages and may hide them or ban chat. We do not open direct messages that have not been reported.
  • Messages from players you block are hidden from you, and they cannot send you direct messages.
  • Please do not post real names, phone numbers, addresses or other account details in chat.

5. Deletion

Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered. Backups and recovery copies are overwritten when their period ends. To delete your game account, email us (section 7); an in-game delete button is in preparation.

6. Cookies and data on your device

The Game also stores saves and settings on your device (the game folder or browser storage) and uses no advertising or tracking cookies. On your first visit the website asks whether we may use analytics cookies. Google Analytics runs only if you choose Allow, with IP addresses shortened. The site works the same if you decline.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data (including account deletion), or ask us to stop using it. Email ashencrown.support@gmail.com with your in-game account code (shown in Settings) and we will act within 10 days.

8. Keeping it safe

  • Traffic between the Game and the server is encrypted (HTTPS). Only the server can read or write the database, and operator functions require an operator key.
  • Sign-ups and emails are kept in an account only the operator can access, protected with two-step verification.
  • Data is deleted within the periods above.

9. Children under 14

Children under 14 may not sign up for playtests. The Game follows Steam's and Google's account rules. If a minor pays without a guardian's consent, the minor or the guardian may ask for cancellation.

10. Contact

Privacy officer: Ashen Crown Studio, ashencrown.support@gmail.com (reply within 2 business days, Korean time).

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we announce the date and the changes on this page and in the Game 7 days before they take effect (30 days if unfavorable to you).
