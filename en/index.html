01 · DEFENSE

Battles where you can stop spells mid-air

Enemy fireballs and curses travel slowly and burst on impact. Whether your party blocks, shoots them down or dodges depends on each companion's class and personality. Five companions fight in real time; skills can run on auto or fire at your command.

  • InterceptArchers shoot projectiles out of the air.
  • CounterspellMages cancel magic with magic.
  • BarrierSupports raise a ward that soaks most of the damage.
  • Shield blockGuardians take it head-on with a shield.
  • CleaveWarriors cut the spell in two.
  • EvadeScouts simply aren't there when it lands.

88 of the 94 companions react in their own way, and the odds of success can be raised with stats, skills and gear.

02 · RELIC DUNGEONS

To grow stronger, you go in yourself

Relic dungeons aren't a menu you tap through. You form an expedition, descend into an underground sanctuary, and find your own way through its halls in first person.

Pressure plates can be disarmed safely if you brought the right class, and the guardian's door only opens once you solve the rune tablet's riddle. Enemies in the halls are fought on the spot; beat the floor's gatekeeper and you choose which door leads down.

  1. Prepare

    Pick five explorers and pack the supply bag. What you pack is only for this run.

  2. Explore in first person

    Map the floor step by step. Watch the floor for plates, the halls for enemies, the walls for hidden paths.

  3. Traps and devices

    Disarm it, or force through and take the damage. Archers and scouts make disarming safe.

  4. Riddles

    Read the tablet, press the right symbol, and the guardian's sealed door opens.

  5. Fight where you stand

    Fights happen right in the corridor, and wounds carry over for the rest of the run.

  6. Rewards and the next floor

    Loot chests and fights, step through the return portal, and head deeper.

Ash Sanctuary, 100 floors · Blood Sanctuary, 150 floors Checkpoint every 10 floors 11 ruins and 8 chapter battles unlock as the story goes on

In development: floor counts, rewards and controls may change during testing.

03 · RAIDS

Ten companions against one boss

Choose a boss and pick ten of your own companions. The boss telegraphs its big attacks, turns to a rage phase as it weakens, and makes a desperate last stand at the end. Fill its Break gauge with skills to cancel the big moves, and finish before the enrage timer runs out.

All ten are companions you raised; this is not online co-op with other players. Raids can be attempted three times a week, with a weekly modifier.

04 · TERRITORY

Rule the lands you win back, and hold them

Nine lands you reclaim in the story gather on one map. Station companions who aren't fighting to boost production, and order them to cultivate, fortify and patrol. When the enemy's threat peaks, an invasion begins: defend the outer gate, the inner gate and the final stand.

  • GarrisonThree companions per land; favored classes give more.
  • AffairsCultivation, defenses and morale, raised by orders to your garrison.
  • ProductionExperience potions and local specialties; higher building levels yield more.
  • InvasionsPick a stratagem (fire, ambush, discord or siege) and defend the gate.

Territory, and invasions in particular, are recent additions; rules and numbers may change.

WORLD

A world of falling ash, and the truth of the crown

The gods are gone, and ash falls from the sky. In a ruined frontier fortress, you and your companions find a shard of the crown, and slowly learn it was never a symbol of power but the seal of an ancient pact between gods and mortals. In the end, you decide what the world becomes.

05 · MONSTERS

The same stage, never the same fight

Every reinforcement wave is a surprise: an ambush from behind, elites with special traits, or a named monster with its own title and signature move. New regions bring new enemies and new traits.

06 · MORE

More to take on

Pick today's destination from the lobby. Every mode has its own bosses and materials, so you'll rebuild your party by the day and week.

  • Weekly bossesSix rotating weekly modifiers, clear ranks from C to SSS.
  • Weekday dungeonsA different elemental king each day. Bring the right counters.
  • BountiesTrade named-hunt tokens for chapter-exclusive gear.
  • ArenaTest your party against other line-ups. Matches against other players' defense teams are still being prepared.
07 · LOOT

At the end of the hunt: Epic and Mythic

Gear drops with a rarity and randomized affixes; no two swords roll the same, and max rolls glow. At the very end waits gear with a story of its own.

Epic sets

MYTHIC

20 Mythics and Manifestation

Every Mythic has its own color and legend. When its power triggers, the figure bound to it appears beside the wearer to strike, shield or heal.

Gather Primal Embers and Mythic Shards from raids and relic dungeons, then craft your Mythic at the forge. Each failure raises your odds on the next attempt.

  • 14 weapon types Each with its own damage range, speed and trait.
  • 52 random affixes Build around bleed, poison, shock or freeze.
  • Enhance up to +15 Higher rarities climb higher.
08 · COMPANIONS

94 companions

Every companion has a faction and a story, and bonds raise their stats. All summon rates and pity thresholds are published in-game, and Crown Gems can be earned steadily through missions, log-ins and achievements.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free to play, with an optional premium currency, Crown Gems, that you can also earn steadily through missions, log-ins and achievements.

Is it single-player? Is there online co-op?

Yes, it's single-player. All ten raid members are your own companions, and the current build has no real-time co-op with other players. Arena matches against other players' defense teams are still being prepared.

When does it launch?

No date yet. It will come to Steam (PC) first as Early Access, with mobile planned later.

Which languages?

Korean and English are planned.

Can I share playtest footage?

Each test's invitation email will tell you. If it doesn't, please ask us first.
