Prices

Ashen Crown: Dawn of the Empty Throne is free to play. These optional purchases are available in the game. Every random outcome is listed on the odds page.

As of 2026-11-19 · Korean won, VAT included · Not on sale yet (pre-release). If prices change at launch, this page is updated first. Prices in other currencies follow Steam's price table.

4. Payment and refunds

  • On Steam, purchases go through Steam only.
  • Products left completely unused can be withdrawn (refunded) within 7 days. Spent gems, passes already started and opened random items cannot be withdrawn; this is shown on the purchase screen in advance.
  • If a minor paid without a guardian's consent, the minor or the guardian may request cancellation.
  • Refunds: Steam Support or ashencrown.support@gmail.com with the purchase date and product name.
