Odds and guarantees

Every random item in the game, with the odds the game itself uses. The numbers below are generated from the game code and match the in-game "Odds & Guarantees" window.

Source: game build 873d6b6 (2026-11-18) · Rates are for the next single pull with no pity progress.

Companion and gear summons cost Crown Gems. Free and purchased Crown Gems have exactly the same odds and pity. Ember Crystals (earned in play) cannot be used to summon.

The per-companion table for every banner and the per-item gear table are long and use Korean item names, so they are on the Korean page.

1. Companion summon

Price: 160 Crown Gems per pull, 1,600 for 10. A 10-pull is the same as ten single pulls, except that your account's first 10-pull guarantees at least one SSR or better.

Rate-up banners

  • When a UR appears, it is the banner's featured UR 50% of the time; otherwise one of the other URs with equal odds.
  • If you get a non-featured UR, your next UR is the featured one. This carries over between banners, so the featured UR takes at most 360 pulls.
  • When an SSR appears, it is the banner's featured SSR 18.75% of the time; otherwise one of the other SSRs with equal odds. Rarity rates do not change.
  • On every banner: featured UR 0.4% (0.8% when guaranteed), featured SSR 1.725%.

  • In a 10-pull, if the first nine give nothing Unique or better, the last is Unique or better (keeping the Unique/Epic/Mythic ratio).
  • The 40th pull without Epic or better is a guaranteed Epic or better. Getting Epic or Mythic resets the count.
  • A Mythic is the featured Mythic 50% of the time. The 200th pull without the featured Mythic guarantees it.
  • Each gear pull gives 1 gear exchange point; 120 points can be exchanged for the current featured Mythic.
  • Gear pity and points are counted separately from companion summons.

3. Mythic crafting
